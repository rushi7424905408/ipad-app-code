FEAT-001 Epic 1: Document Viewport
P0 (Blocker)

Client-Side Vector PDF Canvas & Sub-Pixel Text Selection Engine

Problem Statement
Standard browser readers render flattened text layers that misalign glyph bounding boxes and drop soft hyphens across line breaks, breaking selection of complex German compound words.
Agile User Story
As a German learner reading complex PDF documents,
I want to select any German word or multi-word phrase with sub-pixel precision,
So that I receive instant grammatical analysis and translation without rendering lag.
Verification Checkpoints (Acceptance Criteria)
✓CP-1.1: Loading multi-page PDF renders page 1 with sub-second FCP (≤ 400 ms).
✓CP-1.2: Zooming (50% to 300%) scales canvas and text-layer coordinates with zero offset drift.
✓CP-1.3: Soft-hyphenated words across line breaks cleanly de-hyphenate into unified headwords.
✓CP-1.4: Drag-and-drop of PDF files loads the document immediately without page reload.

FEAT-003 Epic 3: Linguistic Decompiler
P0 (Blocker)

German Morphology Decompiler & Root Stem Extractor (GermanMorphologyEngine)

Problem Statement
German words sharing a root (e.g. antworten, beantworten, verantwortlich, Verantwortung) are scattered in standard dictionaries, preventing learners from seeing conceptual word families.
Agile User Story
As a language student expanding my vocabulary,
I want the system to decompile any word into its complete root family, prefixes, and suffixes,
So that learning one word unlocks 5 to 10 related German words simultaneously.
Verification Checkpoints (Acceptance Criteria)
✓CP-3.1: Selecting 'beantworten' or 'Verantwortung' isolates root 'antwort'.
✓CP-3.2: Selecting 'einführen' or 'Führung' isolates root 'führ'.
✓CP-3.3: Structured into 4 clean categories: Wortfamilie, Präfixe, Trennbar, Suffixe.
✓CP-3.4: In-memory Hash Map caching executes lookups in <0.1 ms.
FEAT-004 Epic 3: Linguistic Decompiler
P0 (Blocker)

Intrinsic Root Guard (Inseparable Prefix Barrier for ge-, be-, etc.)

Problem Statement
Standard stemmers naively strip 'ge-' and 'be-', corrupting intrinsic German roots (gehen ➔ hen, geben ➔ ben, bleiben ➔ leiben) and causing lookup failure.
Agile User Story
As a German learner,
I want the engine to protect intrinsic 'ge-' and 'be-' root verbs from over-stripping,
So that verbs like 'gehen', 'geben', and 'bleiben' resolve to authentic families.
Verification Checkpoints (Acceptance Criteria)
✓CP-4.1: 'gehen' and 'ausgehen' resolve to root 'geh' (10 separable verbs, 4 prefix verbs).
✓CP-4.2: 'geben' and 'aufgeben' resolve to root 'geb' (10 separable verbs, 4 prefix verbs).
✓CP-4.3: 'beantworten' cleanly strips 'be-' without root collisions.
FEAT-005 Epic 3: Linguistic Decompiler
P0 (Blocker)

Descending-Length Separable Verb Combinator & Zero-Hallucination Validator (✂️ Trennbar)

Problem Statement
Unsorted prefix stripping cuts short prefixes (zu-) before compound ones (zusammen-), breaking words like zusammenarbeiten. Other tools hallucinate non-existent separable verbs.
Agile User Story
As a student learning separable verbs,
I want the '✂️ Trennbar' pill to render only when verifiable separable verbs exist for that root,
So that I study verified vocabulary and never memorize hallucinated words.
Verification Checkpoints (Acceptance Criteria)
✓CP-5.1: 'zusammenarbeiten' identifies 'zusammen-' (not 'zu-') and resolves to 'arbeit'.
✓CP-5.2: Verbs with separable forms (lösen, schlagen, führen, kommen) render '✂️ Trennbar'.
✓CP-5.3: Roots with zero separable verbs omit the pill completely.
✓CP-5.4: All separable verbs provide verified English translations.
