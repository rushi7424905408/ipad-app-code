# 🇩🇪 WortSchatz — German PDF Reader & Obsidian Sync

A dedicated, high-performance reader for reading German PDFs with instant German-to-English translation, real-time highlighting, non-occluding margin glossaries, and automatic sync to Obsidian Vaults.

## ✨ Features
- 🖱️ **Double-Click Word & Triple-Click Sentence Translation**
- 🇩🇪 **Authentic German Orthography** (`ä, ö, ü, ß`) with grammatical genders (`der/die/das`)
- 📋 **Full-Text Stacking Margin Glossary** (zero text occlusion)
- 🔮 **Automatic Obsidian Vault Sync** (`vocabulary.md`) under podcast titles
- 🗂️ **Export to Anki (.tsv), CSV, and In-PDF Vector Highlights**
- 📱 **100% iPad & Apple Pencil Support**
- ☁️ **Deployable to Cloudflare Workers / Pages in 30 seconds**

## 🚀 Live Demo
Open `index.html` in your browser or run:
